# Privacy Policy: YT Tidy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

YT Tidy does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores

Only your own settings, saved with Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.sync`: which shelves to hide, how many videos per row, square corners on or off, and whether the sidebar is hidden. Chrome syncs these between your own browsers if Chrome sync is on. The developer cannot see them.

## What the extension does not do

- It runs only on www.youtube.com.
- It does not read, record or send page content, watch history, clicks or keystrokes.
- It makes no network requests and loads no remote code.
- It has no analytics, tracking or ads.
